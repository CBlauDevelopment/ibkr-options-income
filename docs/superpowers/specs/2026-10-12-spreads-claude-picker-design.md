## 1. Intent

The operator wants Claude to make the daily credit-spread decision. It trades at most 1–2 times a
day, so a `claude -p` call per decision is cheap. The deterministic system already gathers the
data and gates every trade. This design adds a reasoning layer that sees everything, weighs a small
menu of gate-approved spreads, and picks one or skips.

**What the operator said:**
- Ingest technical and news data. Fundamentals were mentioned but are dropped here, because they
  carry no information for a same-day SPY spread.
- Propose a few trades, let Claude rank them, and trade its choice.
- Claude drives **paper** orders from day one.
- A simulator that always takes the deterministic pick is tracked alongside, to compare the two.
- The comparison must show *on which days and in which market conditions* each side did better,
  including the data each decision saw.

**Success** means after ~2–3 months (~50+ decisions), `scripts.spreads_report --compare` answers:
"Did Claude's picks beat the deterministic pick after costs, and under which conditions?"
There is **no** assumption that Claude is better. This design exists to measure it.

**Non-goals:**
- No live trading. `run()` still idles under `LIVE_TRADING=true`.
- No Claude influence on sizing, the gate, the trigger, exits, or config.
- No automatic learning loop.

## 2. The invariant, restated for this design

Claude chooses **one letter from a menu of spreads that already passed `risk.validate`**, or
`skip`. It never chooses a strike, a width, a contract count, a price, or an exit. Specifically:

- Every menu item is generated by deterministic code (`selector.py`) and gated and sized by
  `risk.validate` / `risk.size` **before** Claude sees it.
- The service maps the returned letter back to its own candidate object. Anything that is not an
  exact menu letter or `skip` is discarded (→ fallback, §6).
- The chosen spread goes through the existing executor, which re-quotes the legs, re-runs
  `risk.validate` at send time, and ladders the paper order. A hallucinated or malicious response
  can at worst choose a different gate-approved spread, or skip.

## 3. The two books

`spreads.db` already separates books by `SpreadPositionRow.mode`. This adds a third value:

| Book | `mode` | Decided by | Fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| main | `paper` (or `shadow`) per `spreads.mode` | Claude's pick | real paper combo orders (or simulated in `shadow`) |
| baseline | `baseline` | always the deterministic pick | always simulated (existing shadow fill model) |

- Each book has its **own** capital (`starting_capital_usd` + its own realized P&L), trades today,
  open risk, sides today and daily loss. Each is gated against its own state. The two books are
  expected to drift apart: a skipped main-book trigger leaves the main book with a trade the
  baseline already used.
- The `SpreadPosition.mode` literal gains `"baseline"`. Every per-mode store query works unchanged.
- The baseline book exists only when `ai.enabled: true`. With `ai.enabled: false` the system
  behaves exactly as today.

## 4. Decision flow

### 4.1 Morning brief (at `schedule.map_time`, 09:31, after the first GEX map)

`picker.brief(context)` makes one `claude -p` call with no trading deadline
(`brief_timeout_seconds`). It returns a `SpreadDayPlan`:
- `bias` (`bullish` | `neutral` | `bearish`);
- `risk_flags` (list of short strings);
- `skip_conditions` (list of short strings);
- `preferred_distance` (free text, e.g. "≥1.2× EM on puts");
- `summary` (≤ 3 sentences).

It is stored in `spread_day_plans` and posted to the spreads Telegram thread. If the call fails,
the day continues with "no day plan" in the pick prompt. A restart reads the day's plan back from
the table and never re-runs a successful brief.

### 4.2 Menu (when the trigger fires and the 5-minute entry check is due)

`selector.select_menu(chain, levels, cfg, now, sides)` extends today's strike walk. Per allowed side:

1. **Baseline item**: exactly today's `select_candidates` result for that side (the first strike
   beyond the boundary that clears the delta cap and the credit floor).
2. **Inside item**: the next listed strike *closer to spot* than the baseline item, with a long leg
   at `width`. It is included only if it clears the delta cap and the credit floor. It deliberately
   ignores the expected-move/wall boundary: the gate does not check that boundary, and this item
   exists so Claude can be *more* aggressive than the baseline on calm days.
3. **Outer items**: continue the walk outward for up to `selection.menu_size − 2` more strikes that
   clear the credit floor (the walk stops at the first one that doesn't, as today).

`select_candidates` stays as is, a thin wrapper returning the baseline item(s), so the baseline
book and the existing tests keep using it.

**Labels:** within a side, letters run **closest-to-spot first** (`A`, `B`, …). The baseline item
is flagged `baseline: true` and keeps whatever letter its distance gives it. If both sides are
offered (`entry.trigger: always`), puts take letters first, then calls.

Every menu item is run through `risk.validate` against the **main** book's context. Rejected items
are recorded in `spread_candidates`, as today, and dropped from the menu. An empty menu means no
pick call and no main-book trade.

### 4.3 Baseline book

If the baseline item passes `risk.validate` against the **baseline** book's context, it opens
immediately with a simulated fill (`mode="baseline"`). It never waits for Claude. If the baseline
item is missing or fails the baseline gate, the baseline does nothing on this check.

### 4.4 Pick

If the menu is non-empty and the episode hasn't already been decided (§4.5),
`picker.pick(context, plan, menu)` is awaited with `pick_timeout_seconds`. It returns a
`SpreadPick`:
- `choice` (a menu letter or `"skip"`);
- `reason` (one sentence);
- plus metadata: model, effort, latency, notional cost, raw response.

| Result | Main book |
|---|---|
| a menu letter | that candidate → existing executor (re-quote, send-time re-gate, laddered order) |
| `skip` | no order; recorded; Telegram card with the reason |
| off-menu letter, unparseable, CLI missing, timeout | **fallback: the baseline item trades**; recorded with `fallback_reason`; one Telegram alert per reason per day |

### 4.5 Episodes and the call budget

- An **episode** is one arming of the trigger, keyed `(ET day, side, the session extreme that
  armed it)`. A new extreme or a side flip makes a new episode.
- Once an episode has a pick row (taken, skipped or fallback), later entry checks in the same
  episode make **no** pick call. A skip therefore stands until the trigger re-arms.
- In `entry.trigger: always` mode every entry check is its own episode, so only the cap below
  limits calls.
- **Daily cap:** `ai.max_pick_calls_per_day` (default 4), counted from `spread_picks` so it survives
  a restart. At the cap, the main book takes **no new entries** that day (no silent fallback) and
  one Telegram alert is sent. The baseline continues.

### 4.6 Exits

The manage loop runs the existing `evaluate_exit` rules over **both** books' open positions:
- main-book positions close through the executor as today;
- baseline positions close through the shadow close path.

Leg re-quotes are deduplicated by contract, so a main and a baseline position on the same
spread cost no extra lines. Worst case is 2 + 2 positions = 8 legs, within
`max_market_data_lines: 30`.

The pick call runs as an asyncio subprocess (`asyncio.create_subprocess_exec` with a timeout), so
the manage loop keeps running stops, strike-touch exits and the 15:45 time stop while Claude
thinks. The halt file stops new entries in both books; exits keep running.

## 5. Package layout and fences

### 5.1 `src/claude/cli.py` (new, light)

The subprocess retry loop and command builder (`_run_cli`, `_build_cmd`, `_log_cost`) move here
from `src/claude/runner.py`. `runner.py` imports them back, so the wheel's behaviour is unchanged.
It also adds an async single-shot variant for the spreads picker. `cli.py` imports nothing from
the wheel prompt stack, `runner`, `ollama_runner`, or `parser`.

The spreads calls run with:
- `cwd = ai.workdir`, a directory with no CLAUDE.md in any ancestor, so no project context is loaded
  (amended 2026-10-12 at checkpoint A: `claude -p` walks up from its cwd, so the original
  `data/spreads_ai` inside the repo loaded the project `CLAUDE.md`; the default is now
  `~/.cache/ibkr-spreads-ai`, `~` expanded, a relative path resolving under the repo root);
- `--max-turns 1`;
- all tools disallowed and no MCP servers;
- `--model` and `--effort` per call (§7).

### 5.2 `src/spreads_ai/` (new package)

| Module | Job |
|---|---|
| `context.py` | Builds a `SpreadDecisionContext` (see below). Every field degrades to `None` independently; a missing source never blocks a pick |
| `prompts.py` | `build_brief_prompt(ctx)` and `build_pick_prompt(ctx, plan, menu)` (§5.3) |
| `parse.py` | Strict JSON parsing → `SpreadDayPlan` / `SpreadPick`. `choice` must be exactly one of the menu letters or `skip` |
| `picker.py` | `ClaudePicker`, implementing the service's `SpreadPicker` protocol (`brief`, `pick`) over `src.claude.cli` |

`SpreadDecisionContext` holds:
- the GEX levels: regime, flip and distance to it, walls, net GEX;
- the expected move, and the tape: prior close, open, high/low and their times, last price,
  `move_em`, stall minutes, gap %;
- SPY daily technicals through `src/data/` (`analytics.technicals`): price against SMA20/50,
  RSI14, HV30;
- `analytics.market_conditions`: VIX, VIX/VIX3M, 10-year yield and its 5-day change, SPY 5-day
  return;
- today's `risk.events` and `ex_dividend_dates`;
- up to `ai.max_headlines` recent SPY/macro headlines with their deterministic sentiment, read
  through `src.news.store.readonly` + `src.news.store.queries`;
- each book's performance so far (§5.3).

### 5.3 Prompt content requirements

The pick prompt **must** contain the following.

**The objective, stated plainly:** "You are judged on expected P&L after costs over many days,
against a baseline that always takes the item marked baseline. Going further out costs credit,
going closer adds risk, and skipping forfeits the trade's expectancy. None of these is free."

**Per menu item:**
- side, strikes, short delta;
- credit at mid, and credit after modelled costs (commission × 4 legs + slippage);
- costs as % of credit;
- max loss;
- distance from spot in points and in × expected move;
- distance from the nearest wall and the gamma flip;
- the `baseline` flag;
- **three break-even win rates**:
  - at full max loss;
  - under the nominal exits: win = `profit_take_pct` × credit − costs, loss =
    (`stop_debit_multiple` − 1) × credit + costs;
  - the main book's and baseline book's **realized** win rate and break-even win rate
    (`report.compute_stats`), once a book has ≥ 20 closed trades (otherwise marked
    "insufficient history").

**Also:**
- the exit rules;
- the day plan (or "no day plan");
- the context fields from §5.2, with headlines marked as untrusted text;
- the strict JSON reply format.

The brief prompt contains the context (without a menu) and the brief JSON format.

**Why the break-even numbers matter:** the 2026-10-12 latency test showed that a model given only
the full-max-loss break-even reasons that every item has negative edge and skips. Presenting
economics that match the real exits is a correctness requirement, not polish.

## 6. Failure handling

| Failure | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Pick: CLI missing / timeout / unparseable / off-menu letter | Baseline item trades in the main book; `fallback_reason` recorded; one alert per reason per day |
| Brief fails | Pick prompt says "no day plan"; the day continues; recorded with `failed=true` |
| A context source fails (news.db missing, technicals or VIX fetch error) | That field is "unavailable" in the prompt and `NULL` in the snapshot; never blocks |
| Daily pick-call cap reached | Main book: no new entries today; one alert. Baseline continues |
| Send-time re-gate rejects Claude's choice | No order, as today; recorded on the pick row (`send_rejected` + reasons); baseline unaffected |
| Halt file present | No new entries in either book; exits continue |
| Restart mid-day | Day plan, pick count and decided episodes are read back from `spreads.db` |
| `LIVE_TRADING=true` | Unchanged: `run()` idles |

## 7. Configuration

New `ai:` block in `SpreadsCfg` (`src/common/config.py` `SpreadsAiCfg`), in
`config/examples/spreads.yaml` and the operator's private copy:

```yaml
ai:
  enabled: false              # false = exactly today's behaviour: baseline pick only, no baseline book, no Claude calls
  brief_model: claude-opus-5-5
  brief_effort: high
  pick_model: claude-opus-5-5
  pick_effort: high           # measured 2026-10-12: ~16 s wall for a pick-sized prompt; Sonnet/high ~12 s
  brief_timeout_seconds: 120
  pick_timeout_seconds: 45
  max_pick_calls_per_day: 4
  max_headlines: 20
  workdir: ~/.cache/ibkr-spreads-ai  # outside the repo so claude -p loads no project CLAUDE.md (amended 2026-10-12 at checkpoint A; was data/spreads_ai)
selection:
  menu_size: 4                # inside item + baseline + up to 2 outer items
```

Config validation:
- `menu_size ≥ 1`. `1` = baseline item only; `2` = baseline + inside item; `n ≥ 3` = baseline +
  inside + up to `n − 2` outer items. The inside item is offered only when `menu_size ≥ 2`;

- `pick_timeout_seconds` < `schedule.entry_check_minutes × 60`;
- `ai.enabled: true` requires `spreads.enabled: true`.

**Model rationale:** the brief has no deadline and does the heavy synthesis, so it gets the
strongest settings. The pick is the actual trading decision at 2–4 calls a day, so saving tokens
buys nothing, and Opus/high fit the timeout with ~30 s to spare. Per-call settings let the operator
drop the pick to Sonnet/high without a code change.

## 9. Reporting and Telegram

### 9.1 `scripts.spreads_report --compare` (pure math in `src/spreads/report.py`)

- **Headline:** main against baseline. For each: trades, win rate against break-even win rate,
  expectancy, total P&L, max drawdown, and the number of decisions.
- **By decision type:**
  - **took the baseline item:** the books should match apart from fills;
  - **took the inside item / an outer item:** P&L of Claude's choice against the baseline in the
    same episode;
  - **skipped:** what the baseline made or lost in those episodes;
  - **fallback:** reported separately, credited to neither side.
- **Choice distribution:** how often each relative position (inside / baseline / outer-1 /
  outer-2 / skip) is chosen.
- **By condition:** the main−baseline P&L difference grouped by regime, `move_em` bucket, gap day,
  VIX bucket, trend (SPY vs SMA20/50), event flag, side and `plan_bias`. Every row shows *n*, and
  rows with *n* < 5 are marked "noise".
- **Fill bias:** for every main-book paper entry, the simulator's modelled fill next to the real
  fill, so fill quality is separated from choice quality.
- **A sample-size note** is printed while total decisions < 50.
- `--compare --csv PATH`: one row per decision, with the snapshot, menu, choice, reason, both
  books' outcomes and the P&L difference.
- `--day YYYY-MM-DD`: that day's plan and every pick in full (snapshot, menu, prompt text, raw
  response, both books' outcomes).

### 9.2 Telegram (spreads thread, via `src/spreads/notify.py`)

- **09:31:** the brief card (bias, flags, skip conditions, preferred distance, summary).
- **Each pick:** the menu (letter, strikes, delta, credit, baseline flag, gate status), Claude's
  choice and reason, and the baseline action ("baseline: sold B ×N (simulated)"). A fallback is
  flagged loudly.
- **16:10 summary:** two added lines, main vs baseline P&L for today and since the start.

### 9.3 The fence on this evidence

The comparison is read by the operator. Nothing in it feeds the gate, the selector, sizing,
config or the prompts automatically. Realized stats appear in the pick prompt only as displayed
numbers (§5.3), never as a rule.

## 10. Testing

- **Service, with a fake `SpreadPicker`:**
  - each outcome: inside / baseline / outer / skip / off-menu (→ baseline) / timeout (→ baseline)
    / CLI missing (→ baseline);
  - the cap (→ no entry, baseline continues);
  - the books are gated independently (the main book skips, the baseline trades; later the
    main book still has a trade left that the baseline already used);
  - episode dedup (a skip stands until re-arm; a new extreme makes a new episode);
  - a slow pick doesn't block an exit in the same tick window;
  - restart recovery (plan, cap and decided episodes);
  - the halt file stops both books;
  - `ai.enabled: false` matches today's behaviour (the existing spreads suite is unchanged and
    green).
- **Selector:** menu ordering and lettering; the inside item obeys the delta cap and credit floor;
  the outer walk stops at the credit floor; `menu_size: 1` returns the baseline item only; both
  sides under `always`.
- **`spreads_ai`:**
  - parser accepts and rejects the right shapes;
  - prompt snapshot tests asserting the objective statement, all three break-even rates, cost
    lines, the baseline flag and the untrusted-headline marking;
  - context degrades per field.
- **`src.claude.cli`:** `runner.py`'s existing tests stay green after the move; the async variant
  handles timeout and kill.
- **Report:** `--compare` math on a fixed trade log; *n* and noise marking; CSV columns.
- **Fences:** §5.4.
- **Store:** migration adds the columns to an existing `spreads.db` idempotently.
